Who We Are

About Us

LOFT & CO is premium designer furniture in loft, minimalist and Scandinavian modern styles. We create pieces that become the centerpiece of an interior.

We work with solid oak and beech, metal and high-quality fabrics. Every piece goes through quality control, and our designers will help you choose furniture for your space.

Delivery

Within Copenhagen

We'll deliver the furniture to your door at a time that suits you. Cost and timing are confirmed when you place a request.

Across Denmark

We ship to other cities via transport companies. Furniture is securely packed at our warehouse.

Assembly

If you wish, our craftsmen will bring and assemble the furniture on site, so you don't have to do anything yourself.

Peace of Mind

Warranty

  • Warranty on all furniture — under the contract at purchase.
  • Free repair or replacement in case of a manufacturing defect.
